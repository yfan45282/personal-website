Switzerland

Dynamic Cu Exsolution from SrTiO₃ for CO₂ Hydrogenation

Paul Scherrer Institute · Visiting PhD Research

Operando XPS · in situ XRD · Cu exsolution · Oxygen vacancies · CO₂-to-methanol

This project investigates how reducing environments, oxygen-vacancy formation, surface Sr enrichment, and temperature-driven reconstruction regulate Cu exsolution from SrTiO₃ and the resulting CO₂ activation and methanol-synthesis performance.

Zhejiang University

Mechanochemical Preparation of Oxygen-Vacancy Catalysts for Chlorobenzene and NOx Removal

Zhejiang University · Doctoral Research

Mechanochemistry · Mn–Ce oxides · Oxygen circulation · NH₃-SCR · CVOC oxidation

This research develops ball-milling and interfacial engineering strategies to create oxygen vacancies and dynamic redox centers, enabling synergistic removal of chlorobenzene and NOx while improving reaction efficiency and sulfur tolerance.

South China University of Technology

Microorganism-Assisted Nitrogen Doping of Porous Carbon for VOC Adsorption

South China University of Technology · Master’s Research

Biochar · Microbial nitrogen fixation · Pore engineering · VOC adsorption

This work uses microorganism-assisted nitrogen fixation and pore-structure regulation to prepare nitrogen-doped porous carbon, clarifying how nitrogen functionalities and hierarchical porosity enhance VOC adsorption capacity and selectivity.
