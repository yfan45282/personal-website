About

Researching catalysts as evolving systems—not static materials.

I am a PhD candidate in Power Engineering and Engineering Thermophysics at the School of Energy Engineering, Zhejiang University, supervised by Prof. Shengyong Lu. I am currently conducting visiting doctoral research in the group of Prof. Jeroen A. van Bokhoven at the Paul Scherrer Institute.

My research combines catalyst design, fixed-bed catalytic evaluation, operando XPS, in situ XRD, in situ DRIFTS, chemisorption, electron microscopy, and computation-assisted analysis to reveal how oxygen vacancies, metal exsolution, and surface reconstruction control catalytic performance.
