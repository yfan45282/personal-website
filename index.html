About

Researching catalysts as evolving systems—not static materials.

My research focuses on the dynamic response of heterogeneous catalysts to realistic reaction environments. I combine catalyst design, fixed-bed evaluation, operando XPS, in situ XRD, in situ DRIFTS, chemisorption, electron microscopy, and computation-assisted analysis.

I am pursuing a PhD in Power Engineering and Engineering Thermophysics at the School of Energy Engineering, Zhejiang University, supervised by Prof. Shengyong Lu. At PSI, I conduct visiting doctoral research in the group of Prof. Jeroen A. van Bokhoven.

Research

Selected research themes

My work connects dynamic catalyst structure with catalytic function across environmental and energy-related reactions.

01

Dynamic Catalyst Evolution

Revealing catalyst reconstruction and active-site evolution under realistic reaction conditions.

  • Operando XPS
  • in situ XRD
  • in situ DRIFTS
02

Oxygen-Vacancy Chemistry

Understanding vacancy formation, migration, replenishment, and interfacial oxygen circulation.

  • Defect engineering
  • Redox cycles
  • Structure–activity relations
03

Cu Exsolution & CO₂ Conversion

Investigating Cu/SrTiO₃ surface reconstruction and CO₂ hydrogenation to methanol.

  • Metal exsolution
  • Perovskites
  • CO₂ hydrogenation
04

Multi-Pollutant Catalysis

Developing Mn–Ce- and Ru-containing catalysts for simultaneous removal of NOx and chlorinated VOCs.

  • NH₃-SCR
  • CVOC oxidation
  • Mechanochemistry
