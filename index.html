Fan Yao

PhD Candidate at Zhejiang University · Visiting PhD Researcher at Paul Scherrer Institute

I study how catalysts evolve under realistic reaction conditions. My work combines catalyst synthesis, operando spectroscopy, in situ characterization, and mechanistic analysis to understand oxygen vacancies, metal exsolution, surface reconstruction, and interfacial redox chemistry.
