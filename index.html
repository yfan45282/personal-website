Hello, welcome to my academic homepage.

I am a researcher in heterogeneous catalysis, using operando spectroscopy and defect engineering to understand dynamic catalyst evolution.

My research investigates how oxygen vacancies, metal exsolution, interfacial redox processes, and surface reconstruction govern catalytic performance under realistic reaction conditions. I work across environmental catalysis and CO₂ conversion.

Research at a glance

Research

Dynamic catalyst evolution, oxygen-vacancy chemistry, Cu exsolution, and interfacial reaction mechanisms.

Characterization

Operando XPS, in situ XRD, in situ DRIFTS, chemisorption, electron microscopy, and synchrotron methods.

Catalytic Systems

Mn–Ce and Ru-containing oxides, Cu/SrTiO₃ perovskites, NH₃-SCR, CVOC oxidation, and CO₂ hydrogenation.

Academic Profile

Four published first-author papers, PSI visiting research, and multiple oral conference presentations.

Research

Selected themes

Dynamic Catalyst Evolution

Tracking catalyst reconstruction and active-site evolution under realistic reaction environments.

Oxygen-Vacancy Chemistry

Understanding vacancy formation, migration, replenishment, and interfacial oxygen circulation.

Cu Exsolution and CO₂ Conversion

Investigating Cu/SrTiO₃ surface reconstruction and CO₂ hydrogenation to methanol.

Synergistic Multi-Pollutant Catalysis

Developing catalysts for simultaneous removal of NOx and chlorinated VOCs with improved sulfur tolerance.
