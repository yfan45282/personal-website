Research Interests

My research focuses on understanding how heterogeneous catalysts dynamically evolve under realistic reaction conditions. I combine catalyst design, operando spectroscopy, advanced characterization, and mechanistic analysis to reveal the roles of oxygen vacancies, metal exsolution, interfacial redox processes, and surface reconstruction.

Current topics include dynamic catalyst evolution, operando XPS, in situ XRD, in situ DRIFTS, Cu/SrTiO₃ exsolution, CO₂ hydrogenation, synergistic NOx–CVOC catalysis, sulfur resistance, and mechanochemical synthesis.
