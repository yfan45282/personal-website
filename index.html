Heterogeneous Catalysis · Operando Spectroscopy

Hello! This is Fan Yao.

I am a Ph.D. researcher in heterogeneous catalysis with a passion for uncovering catalytic mechanisms through advanced characterization and materials design. My research focuses on operando catalysis, oxygen vacancy chemistry, dynamic active-site evolution, and sustainable catalytic processes for environmental remediation and energy conversion. I am currently conducting research at the Paul Scherrer Institute (PSI), Switzerland, where I explore structure–activity relationships using synchrotron-based techniques and operando spectroscopy.
